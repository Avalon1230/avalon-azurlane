## Repeatable maintenance procedure

1. Read a small selected set of current Koumakan indexes/pages through permitted access. Cache requests, identify the client, respect maxlag and Retry-After, and stop on access/rate limits. Record revision IDs, timestamps and attribution.
2. Cross-check new schedules and availability against official regional notices and verified client records. Record contradictions and source gaps. Do not silently fill unknown regions.
3. Match immutable identities and occurrences before adding data. Preserve historical links, aliases, date precision and retrospective verification timestamps. Never derive a permanent release from age or a rerun.
4. Update the independently reviewed `data/events/backfill-evidence.json` facts and runtime catalog together. The offline audit checks agreement, not live source freshness.
5. Scope rewards, missions, shops and drops to explicitly verified regional occurrences. No original reward transfers to War Archives automatically.
6. Run `node scripts/audit-event-catalog.mjs`, Node/syntax/source suites and controlled-clock browser tests. Inspect collapsed/expanded filters, four sections, history and regional summaries at 360/390/1280px.
7. Update this ledger, screenshots and the permanent status handoff in a small source-audited PR. Independent QA precedes Commander authorization; never auto-merge or deploy.
